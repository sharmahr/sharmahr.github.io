04 2026 Open source

zed-db From scratch

A persistent relational database engine written in Python, because the fastest way to understand Postgres is to rebuild a small one. Hand-written SQL parser, B-tree storage with disk persistence, hash and range indexes, inner/left/right/cross joins, aggregates with GROUP BY, and BEGIN/COMMIT/ROLLBACK transactions — with a REPL that reports whether a query took a sequential scan or an index scan.
