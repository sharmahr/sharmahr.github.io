Privacy policy

Hot Diff Uploader · effective November 2, 2026

Hot Diff Uploader (the "app") is a personal tool, operated by Hardik Sharma, that uploads videos to the Hot Diff YouTube channel. Only the channel's owner signs in to it.

What the app accesses

With your permission, through the YouTube Data API, the app reads your channel's id, name and list of uploads, and the status and comments of videos it uploaded; it uploads videos, sets their thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, and posts comments on them.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to upload and publish your videos and to check that they went up. Sign-in tokens are stored only in a private file on the computer that runs the app. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Hot Diff Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the stored tokens by deleting the file that holds them.
