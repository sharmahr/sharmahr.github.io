Hot Diff Uploader

Hot Diff Uploader is a personal tool that uploads the videos made for the Hot Diff YouTube channel (tech news, minus the nuance) to that channel. It runs on its owner's computer and is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's permission it uploads videos, sets their thumbnails and schedules, and posts image-credit comments on them, using the YouTube Data API.
